## Using the name

Write **Insider Threat and Risk Architecture (INTRA&trade;)** in full the
first time it appears, in documentation, on a website, in a repository, in a
publication, in a presentation, in training material, and in anything
describing a product. INTRA on its own is fine afterwards, where the context
carries it.

Do not alter the name, and do not set it in a way that suggests a different
owner or a different source.

## What using it does not mean

Using the framework, its taxonomy, its identifiers, its content, or anything
mapped to it does not make the resulting work sponsored, endorsed, approved,
certified, or supervised by Alessandro Aledda, and does not make it
affiliated with him.

Do not say or imply otherwise unless he has agreed to it in writing.

## Names of things you build

Do not take the name, or a name close enough to be confused with it, into the
name of a product, a company, a service, a certification, a package, a
repository, a domain, or an account, where doing so could leave anyone
mistaken about who owns or stands behind the thing.

Describing what your work does is a different matter, and is welcome where it
is true and does not imply endorsement.

Fine:

- Compatible with INTRA&trade;
- Mapped to INTRA&trade; controls
- Detection content aligned to Insider Threat and Risk Architecture

Not fine, without written agreement:

- Official INTRA&trade; Platform
- INTRA&trade; Certified
- INTRA&trade; Detection Suite
- Aledda-approved INTRA&trade; integration

endorsement of any product, service, or organization.
